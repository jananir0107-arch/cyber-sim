🛡️ What is Incident Response?

Incident response is the process of identifying, analyzing, containing and recovering from a cyber security incident.

🔄 Incident Response Activities

🔍 Detect

Identify suspicious activity or a cyber attack.

🧠 Analyze

Investigate the incident and understand its impact.

🔌 Contain

Isolate the affected system and stop the threat.

🛠️ Recover

Restore the affected system and return to normal.

🛡️ Incident Response Tips

  • Stay calm when an incident occurs.
  • Identify the affected system.
  • Collect and preserve evidence.
  • Isolate affected systems.
  • Report the incident to the security team.
  • Recover systems safely.
